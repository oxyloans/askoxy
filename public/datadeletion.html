Privacy & Data Protection

AskOxy – User Data Deletion Instructions

AskOxy respects user privacy and provides users with the ability to request deletion of their personal information.

1

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of your personal data:

  1. 1
    Send an email to support@askoxy.ai.
  2. 2
    Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. 3
    Include your registered email address or WhatsApp phone number associated with your AskOxy account.
  4. 4
    Our support team will verify your request and process it.

2

What information can be deleted?

Depending on your request, this may include:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Personal information associated with your account.
  • WhatsApp-related user information stored by AskOxy.
  • Other personal data associated with your account, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
3

Processing your request

We will verify your identity, review the request and delete eligible personal data in accordance with applicable laws and our privacy policy. We will communicate with you if additional information is required.
